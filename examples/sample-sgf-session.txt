Stonehue SGF save and load session
===================================

This transcript shows the record commands on a 9 by 9 board.
The engine writes "= " for success. It writes "? " for an error.

The engine plays three moves and saves the game. Then it loads
the record back and shows the recovered position.

Commands and replies
--------------------

boardsize 9
komi 6.5
play b D4
play w F4
play b D6
printsgf /tmp/stonehue-demo.sgf
printsgf
= (;FF[4]GM[1]SZ[9]KM[6.5];B[df];W[ff];B[dd])
clear_board
loadsgf /tmp/stonehue-demo.sgf
showboard
=
   A B C D E F G H J
 9 . . . . . . . . .
 8 . . . . . . . . .
 7 . . . . . . . . .
 6 . . . X . . . . .
 5 . . . . . . . . .
 4 . . . X . O . . .
 3 . . . . . . . . .
 2 . . . . . . . . .
 1 . . . . . . . . .

final_score
= W+5.5
quit

The engine ignores the "#" lines. Read them as comments.

The printed record uses the Smart Game Format. Standard Go editors
such as Sabaki and GoGui can open this file.
